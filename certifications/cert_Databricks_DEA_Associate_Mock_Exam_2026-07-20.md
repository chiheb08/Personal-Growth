### A. Lakehouse Platform & Architecture

**Q1.** **Answer: B**

- **Why:** Lakehouse = data lake flexibility + warehouse guarantees, typically via Delta Lake on object storage.

- **Why:** Control plane orchestrates; data plane holds compute + storage. Table files are in your cloud storage.

**Q3.** **Answer: B**
